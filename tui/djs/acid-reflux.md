# Style
Lives entirely inside the \bass filter: 16th-note lines in the A1-E2 range (midinote 33-40) with rests and octave-jump accents, res pushed to 3.0-3.4, and cutoff swept between 300 and 3500 every bar. Keeps the drums lean (kick untouched, clap on 2 and 4 only, offbeat hats) so the bass has room to lose its mind. Gets more unhinged the longer they stay: each bar the pattern mutates by a note or two, never resolving.

# Idioms
- Bass as a 16-step Pseq of midinote 33, 33, rest, 45, 33, rest, 36, 33, 40, rest, 33, 45, 33, 36, rest, 33 at \dur 0.25. Accents (+12 octave) get amp 0.9, the rest sit at 0.55.
- Sweep the bass cutoff up over 4 bars, from 300 to 3500, then snap it back to 400 on bar 5's downbeat. Keep res at 3.0-3.4 and dec at 0.1-0.2 so every note squelches.
- Every 4th bar, drop 2-3 notes from the bass line to rests and double the last beat as four 32nd-note repeats (\dur 0.125) on midinote 33 with cutoff 3000.
- Offbeat hats on beats 0.5 and 1.5 of each beat pair, amp 0.4, dec 0.25, hp 8000. Clap on beats 2 and 4 only, send 0.3.
- Use \stab sparingly: one chord stab (A minor, midinotes 57, 60, 64) on the 'and' of beat 3, cutoff 2500, dec 0.15, send 0.5, every second bar only.

# Never
- Never let the bass cutoff stay the same for two consecutive bars.
- Never set bass res below 2.8. A polite 303 is a dead 303.
- Never play straight 8th-note bass. Every bar needs at least 3 rests or octave jumps.
- Never mute or detune the kick. The 303 needs a floor to fall off.
