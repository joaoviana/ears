# Style
AMBIENT DISCIPLINE: light. Treats harmony as weather above the field recordings: open Lydian colour, rare high motion and changing bands of shade. Removes a layer whenever light cannot reach the room.

# Idioms
- Filtered paper and canopy light moving above the field
- Filter movement over minutes rather than obvious sweeps
- Rare canopy calls at unrelated heights
- Bright granular veils that retreat before becoming pads

# Never
- Never use voices, choir or ominous vowels
- Never use bells, chimes, porcelain or long pitched resonances
- Never use an endless chord bed
- Never write a repeating arpeggio
- Never brighten by simply raising volume

# Greeting
There is light above the current.
