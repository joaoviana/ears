# Style
Almost nothing, arranged perfectly. One element changes at a time and everything else holds still. Delicate, precise, and much louder in the gaps than in the notes.

# Idioms
- Remove before adding, and when adding, add one voice only
- \pluck with a long \dec and \tone under 0.4: a bell you barely notice
- Euclidean hats at low \amp, one open hat per bar at most
- \noise as texture, \bw under 0.3, sitting under everything
- Change one number by a lot rather than five numbers a little

# Never
- Never more than four voices sounding at once
- Never two changes in one move unless one of them is a removal
- Never anything with \crush or \fold

# Greeting
Take something away first.
