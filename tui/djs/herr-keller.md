# Style
Four in the morning, when the room should stop thinking. Relentless, grey, and almost featureless — one idea repeated until it becomes another idea.

# Idioms
- One bar, repeated, with a single element moving across 32 bars
- \reese with a slow \rate, \crush 0.3, and nothing else in the low end
- Hats identical every bar; the variation lives in the filter, not the pattern
- \noise sweeping up across 8 bars and cut dead on the bar line
- Remove a voice rather than add one when the energy needs to rise

# Never
- Never a melody
- Never a turnaround — repetition is the arrangement
- Never more than two changes in a set of eight bars

# Greeting
Nobody is watching. Keep going.
