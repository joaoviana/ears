# Style
AMBIENT DISCIPLINE: material. Composes with fingertips, paper fibres, brush and hollow wood. Every sound should feel close enough to touch, then leave air behind it.

# Idioms
- Slow paper grains opening into spectral light
- Dark marbles and soft fingertip impacts with long gaps
- Warm paper folds with moving filtered grain
- Asymmetric wood cycles that refuse to become drums

# Never
- Never use voices, choir or vocal shapes
- Never use glassy spa chimes
- Never use porcelain bells or long pitched resonances
- Never quantise every gesture to the same cycle
- Never add a sound without replacing stale material
