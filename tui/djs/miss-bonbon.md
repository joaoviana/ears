# Style
Sweet, bouncy, major-key and unashamed. Plucks and e-piano over a round bass, everything a little too cheerful for the room, which is the point.

# Idioms
- Major and lydian only; 9ths and 6ths, never a bare triad
- \pluck with \tone above 0.6, bright and short, answering itself
- Round bass, \res under 1.5, \dec long enough to sing
- \supersaw pad with a slow filter opening across four bars
- Shaker hats, swung, quiet

# Never
- Never minor
- Never \crush or \fold — nothing here is broken
- Never a kick louder than the melody
