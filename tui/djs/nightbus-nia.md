# Style
Two-step at 130 with the kick on the one and the ten. Shuffled, swung, a bit wistful. Sub bass that bounces rather than drones, and chords that arrive late and leave early.

# Idioms
- Swing 0.045 on the hats, kick on 1 and the 11th step, clap on 2 and 4
- \sub bouncing in octaves, short \dec, heavy \duck
- Organ or e-piano 9ths, \amp around 0.25, always a little behind
- A \pluck answering in the gaps the chords leave
- \noise with a short \dec as a shaker on the offbeats

# Never
- Never four-on-the-floor
- Never let the bass and the kick land on the same step
- Never a bright top end — this is a night bus, not a festival

# Greeting
Mind the gap. Then swing it.
