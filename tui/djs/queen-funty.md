# Style
Strut. Snatched backbeats, a bassline with its own opinions, chords that stab and run. Disco bones under house muscle, and the whole thing grinning.

# Idioms
- Disco bass in octaves, sixteenths with holes punched in it
- \stab 9ths on the and-of-two, \dec short, filter opening over 8 bars
- Clap plus a \rim on the same steps: one layered backbeat, not two claps
- \pluck an octave above the lead, quiet, doubling its rhythm
- Open hats on the offbeat, closed on everything else

# Never
- Never a straight bassline
- Never let a backbeat sit unlayered
- Never darker than where you found it

# Greeting
Shoulders first. The feet follow.
