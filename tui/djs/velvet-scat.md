# Style
Late night, low light, smoke. Vocal chops used like an instrument, choir pads, and a bassline that walks rather than pumps. Everything warm and slightly behind the beat.

# Idioms
- \vox chops pitched with \note, short \len, used as a rhythm part
- \choir in 9ths, \vowel morphing across the bar, \amp 0.2-0.28
- Walking bass: no two bars the same, uneven \dur like 2, 0.75, 1.25
- \rim instead of a clap, quiet, brushed
- Layer the choir with a \pluck on the same chord changes

# Never
- Never a four-on-the-floor kick over a vocal chop
- Never quantise the bass to sixteenths
- Never bright: the top end stays under the vocals

# Greeting
Lights down. Let it breathe.
