# Nature recordings

The bundled WAV files are short, level-controlled excerpts made for this project from public-domain recordings.
They were resampled to 32 kHz stereo and faded at both ends. No attribution is required; the sources are recorded
here so their provenance remains clear.

| Local file | Source | Creator | Licence | Local transformation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rain.wav` | [Rain.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain.ogg) | ジダネ | Public domain | full 10-second recording; high-pass and fades |
| `birds.wav` | [Bird singing.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bird_singing.ogg) | jc | Public domain | 32-second excerpt from 00:04; high-pass and fades |
| `waves.wav` | [Waves.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Waves.ogg) | Dsw4 | Public domain | 36-second excerpt from 00:24; band-limit and fades |

The source pages contain the public-domain declarations and original files.
